About Shop Gate

Fit Shelter started with a simple observation and a deep passion. We felt a gap in the local scene and were driven to bring something different, something authentic to our community. Founded in February 2025, Fit Shelter was built from the belief that thrift is a culture. It's about the stories, textures, and identities that travel from one person to another. When you pick up a piece at Fit Shelter, you're not just getting clothing; you're inheriting a memory, a sound, and a history. We built Fit Shelter to cultivate the thrift culture in Saudi Arabia. We are dedicated to connecting this movement with style, music, and a vibrant local community. We're not just opening a store; we're building a culture that moves.
